Projects Done In Polytechnic

Time Period: 2018–2021
Course: Diploma in Game Development and Technology
School: Nanyang Polytechnic

Final Year Project (2020)

Done in collaboration with Tan Tock Seng Hospital, developed in Unity to spread awareness of Covid-19 at the height of the pandemic.

Undertook the role of the lead programmer, worked with designers and a programmer to make a game that is based on Plague Inc, where players are to mitigate the spread of the virus instead of spreading it. Made with the purpose of spreading awareness about the different ways to protect oneself against the virus.

Independent Work Project (2020)

Done as a graded solo project with the intent of trying to recreate a fighting game. Developed in Unity to make a simple fighting game loop.
